1. Crontab
 is a Linux utility used to schedule jobs to run automatically at specific times.
2. We schedule jobs like backups, log cleanup, monitoring, and maintenance tasks.
3. Jobs are often scheduled during low-traffic periods to minimize performance impact.
4. `crontab -e` – Edit and schedule cron jobs.
5. `crontab -l` – List the current user's scheduled cron jobs.
6. `crontab -r` – Remove all cron jobs for the current user.
7. Cron syntax: `MIN HOUR DAY MONTH DAY_OF_WEEK COMMAND`.
8. `0 2 * * * /path/backup.sh` – Run a backup script daily at 2:00 AM.
9. `sudo tail -f /var/log/cron` – Monitor cron logs on Amazon Linux and many RHEL-based systems.
10. `*/5 * * * * /path/script.sh` – Execute a script every five minutes.
