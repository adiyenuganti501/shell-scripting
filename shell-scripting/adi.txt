Ramayana is an ancient Indian epic.
Lord Rama was the prince of Ayodhya.
Sita was the wife of Lord Rama.
Lakshmana was the younger brother of Rama.
Hanuman was a great devotee of Lord Rama.
Ravana was the king of Lanka.
Rama went to the forest for fourteen years.
Sita and Lakshmana accompanied Rama.
Ravana kidnapped Sita and took her to Lanka.
Hanuman crossed the ocean to find Sita.
Hanuman found Sita in Ashoka Vatika.
Rama built a bridge to reach Lanka.
Rama fought against Ravana.
Rama defeated Ravana and rescued Sita.
Rama returned to Ayodhya.
The people of Ayodhya welcomed Rama.
Ramayana teaches us about truth and duty.
Lord Rama is known for his courage.
Hanuman is known for his strength and devotion.
Truth always wins over evil.
